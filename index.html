Common questions

When will analysisACE open?

The features on this page are built and being tested with demo data. Leave your email below and we will tell you the day it opens.

Is this investment advice?

No. analysisACE shows historical analysis for education. It never recommends buying, selling or holding anything, and the decision is always yours.

Which Shariah method do you use?

Two: an AAOIFI-style and an S&P-style method, shown side by side. The thresholds are published and versioned, and they stay provisional until a Shariah advisor approves them.

Where does the data come from?

Prices and indices from the stock exchange, rates and macro figures from Bangladesh Bank and other public sources, and company figures from annual and quarterly reports. Every figure names its source.

Is my household's data private?

Yes. Only members of your household can see it. Account numbers and documents are encrypted, optional uses need your consent, and you can export or delete your data at any time.

Can I use it in Bangla?

Yes. Every page, notice and PDF statement is available in Bangla and English, with Bangla digits.
