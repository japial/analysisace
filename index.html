FIRE, explained

Financial independence in three ideas

FIRE means Financial Independence, Retire Early. You are financially independent when your invested capital could pay your family's yearly costs. Retiring early is optional; the freedom is the point.

  1. 01 / NUMBER

    Your FIRE number

    Add up a year of your household's real costs and multiply by 25. That is roughly the invested capital that could pay for those costs every year at a 4% withdrawal rate.

  2. 02 / RATE

    Your savings rate

    The share of income you keep. It works twice: it builds capital faster, and it lowers the costs your capital has to cover.

  3. 03 / DATE

    Your FIRE date

    The age at which your investable capital is projected to reach your FIRE number, from your own savings, returns and inflation. Progress updates as your records grow.

FIRE in Bangladesh

A FIRE plan made for life here

Most FIRE calculators assume low inflation and an index fund. Families here face different facts, and the plan handles them.

  • Inflation from BBS figures

    Your plan starts from the last 12 months of the BBS consumer price index, and your costs are shown in today's prices.

  • Returns from halal choices

    The starting return comes from your own sukuk and Islamic deposits. Shares can be checked against a Shariah screen with reasons.

  • The people you look after

    Money sent to parents and family support is recorded as a cost, so your FIRE number includes everyone you provide for.

  • Land and home kept apart

    Land and the family home are left out of the money you can live from by default, because they cannot be sold a piece at a time. You can include them.

  • Zakat inside the plan

    Zakat is worked out line by line each year, and the Zakat and sadaqah you record as costs are part of your FIRE number.

  • Tested against bad years

    See your plan after a market fall in year one, higher costs, higher inflation, stopping work five years sooner, or a replay of a past DSE event.

FAQ

Common questions

What is FIRE?

Financial Independence, Retire Early. It means having enough invested that its returns could pay your yearly costs, so paid work becomes a choice. Many people keep working after they reach it.

Do I have to retire early?

No. Most people use FIRE as a target for freedom: to change careers, start a business, care for family or work less. The plan shows when that choice could open up.

Is my FIRE age a promise?

No. It is a projection from the figures you enter and assumptions about returns and inflation. Markets and costs change, so the plan includes stress tests and you can update it at any time.

What does ACE mean?

Assets, Capital and Earnings. Assets are what you own and owe. Capital is the part of your money that is invested or put to work. Earnings are your income, costs and savings. Together they decide how soon you could reach FIRE.

Do I need to own shares?

No. A FIRE plan can be built on savings certificates, sukuk, Islamic deposits, gold and other assets. Share tools are an extra for people who hold DSE stocks.

Where does the data come from?

Your household figures come from what you enter or import. Market figures come from the stock exchange, Bangladesh Bank and other public sources, and company reports. Every figure names its source.

Is my household's data private?

Yes. Only members of your household can see it. Account numbers and documents are encrypted, optional uses need your consent, and you can export or delete your data at any time.

Can I use it in Bangla?

Yes. Every page, notice and PDF statement is available in Bangla and English, with Bangla digits.
